Since officially confirming development in 2022, Rockstar Games has released information about GTA6 through the Newswire, its website, and social media. What follows is a timeline of the key official statements and milestones up to September 2026.

## Development Confirmation and Early Trailers

In February 2022, Rockstar first confirmed that the next Grand Theft Auto was in development.

In December 2023, Trailer 1 arrived, revealing the modern-day Vice City setting and the dual protagonists Jason and Lucia, and setting a “Fall 2025” release window.

In May 2025, Trailer 2 launched, showing more of the characters' relationships, map regions, and gameplay details.

![Development Confirmation and Early Trailers](/media/f4be27339c97.webp)

## Release Date Changes

On November 6, 2025, Rockstar issued a major statement:

> “Grand Theft Auto VI will now release on Thursday, November 19, 2026.

> We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has been a long wait, but these extra months will allow us to finish the game with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve.”

The window, first set for fall 2025 and later moved to May 2026, was finally locked to November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

![Release Date Changes](/media/4975645f83b8.webp)

## Pre-Orders and Pricing

On June 18, 2026, Rockstar revealed the cover art and announced that pre-orders would open at midnight on June 25.

The standard edition is priced at $79.99 and the ultimate edition at $99.99.

Everyone who buys before November 20 receives the “Vintage Vice City Pack” pre-order bonus. Digital versions support pre-loading on November 12, while physical copies come as a download code in the box.

## The Extended Look and Gameplay Showcase

On August 6, 2026, Rockstar announced that Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look would premiere on August 27. The video debuted exclusively on Netflix first, then arrived on the official YouTube channel and website.

The official description read:

“Captured entirely from in-game footage on PlayStation 5, this extended look at Grand Theft Auto VI showcases the biggest, most immersive evolution of the series yet.”

It is the most comprehensive official gameplay showcase to date, running about 27 minutes and covering missions, driving, combat, everyday activities, and dual-protagonist co-op.

![The Extended Look and Gameplay Showcase](/media/596471987b99.webp)

## Response to the Leak

In late August 2026, after gameplay leaks from CyberLeek and others, Rockstar issued a statement calling the material “heartbreaking,” saying it was not how the team wanted players to first experience the game, and reiterating that the November 19 release date was unchanged.

## The Album and Other Official Content

On September 17, 2026, Rockstar and Atlantic Records announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, featuring 34 original tracks, arriving alongside the game's release date with a physical edition as well.

The studio has also kept updating the Newswire with pre-order details, platform confirmations, and companion content, while making clear that launch is a single-player experience and that the online mode will follow later.

![The Album and Other Official Content](/media/ef8da6f17edd.webp)

## Platforms and What Comes Next

Officially, only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are confirmed for launch. No specific timing has been announced for PC. Take-Two management has repeatedly restated the release date and the single-player positioning of the launch in public, and said GTA Online will continue to run.

## Summary

Rockstar's official communication stays restrained and precise. From Trailer 1 to the final locked-in date, and on to the in-depth Extended Look, every step has revolved around one principle: polish the game to the standard the series deserves. As of now, all public information points to a November 19, 2026 release, and players can pre-order through official channels and look forward to the full experience.

For the latest official updates, follow Rockstar Games Newswire and the official site at rockstargames.com/VI.
