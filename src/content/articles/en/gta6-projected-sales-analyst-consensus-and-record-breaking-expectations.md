GTA6 has not launched yet, but industry expectations for its sales have already reached unprecedented heights. Across reports from independent research firms and investment banks, the mainstream forecast clusters around 30 to 50 million copies in the first year, with the first week possibly setting a historical record. What follows draws on public analysis from mid-to-late 2026 to lay out the most representative sales projections available.

## A Roundup of Mainstream Analyst Forecasts

| **Firm / Analyst** | **Forecast Sales** | **Time Window** | **Implied Revenue** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DFC Intelligence | ~40 million | First year | ~$3.2 billion |
| Joost van Dreunen | ~38 million | First 12 months | ~$3.0 billion |
| Ampere Analysis | 30–35 million | End of launch year | — |
| Piper Sandler | ~35 million | Through April 2027 (~4.5 months) | — |
| Morgan Stanley | ~40 million | First ~4 months (FY2027) | — |
| Newzoo (aggressive) | 37–51 million | First week | $3.3–5.2 billion |
| Alinea Analytics | ~30 million (first week) → 50 million | First week to early 2027 | — |
| Enders Analysis (bullish) | Over 50 million, possibly close to 60 million | First year | — |

All of these figures are derived from the current PS5 and Xbox Series X|S install base (roughly 120–130 million units), pre-order momentum and GTA5's historical performance.

![A Roundup of Mainstream Analyst Forecasts](/media/0db070a8a33f.webp)

## First Week and First Month: Potentially a New Entertainment Industry Record

First-week performance draws the most attention. Using a pre-order curve model, Newzoo projects that GTA6 could sell between 37 and 51 million copies in its first week, implying roughly $3.3 to $5.2 billion in revenue. Even at the conservative end, that far exceeds GTA5's $1 billion record in its first three days.

Other firms take a more conservative view of the first day and first week:

- DFC Intelligence expects about 25 million on day one, including pre-orders.
- Some analysts consider 30 million in the first week entirely possible.

On the current console install base, a first week above 40 million copies would mean more than 30% of existing next-gen console owners buying the game, an attachment rate that is extremely rare for a big-budget AAA release.

## How the First Year Maps to Take-Two's Fiscal Targets

Take-Two's official FY2027 net bookings target (the year ending March 31, 2027) is $8.0–8.2 billion, about 20% growth over FY2026. The company states plainly that the growth comes mainly from GTA6.

Analysts generally read that target as GTA6 selling 35 to 40 million copies within about 4.5 months of launch. The forecasts from Morgan Stanley and Piper Sandler both fall in that range, and DFC Intelligence's “40 million copies and $3.2 billion in the first year” fits the same framework.

Note that Take-Two's figure covers company-wide bookings rather than GTA6 alone. After stripping out other businesses, GTA6's actual contribution is expected to land in the $1.3–2.0 billion range in net terms, after platform cuts.

## Comparison With GTA5

| **Metric** | **GTA5 (2013)** | **GTA6 (projected)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day-one/first-week sales | ~11–16 million | 25–51 million |
| First-month sales | ~29 million | most forecasts above 30 million |
| First-year sales | ~45–50 million (estimated) | 38–50 million |
| Lifetime sales (as of 2026) | over 230 million | unknown |
| Launch platforms | previous-gen consoles + later ports | next-gen consoles only (at launch) |

GTA5's enormous lifetime sales owe a great deal to the later PC release, the next-gen remaster and more than a decade of GTA Online. GTA6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series only, on a relatively limited install base, so expectations ride on its short-term explosion, while whether it can overtake its predecessor over the long run remains disputed.

## The Key Variables That Will Move the Numbers

1. Price: a $79.99 standard edition and a $99.99 ultimate edition sit above the usual AAA norm, which may hold back some price-sensitive buyers, but pre-order data shows a high share of the premium version.

2. Platform limits: no PC release at launch, so the short-term ceiling is set by the console install base.

3. Pre-order conversion: CEO Strauss Zelnick called the pre-orders “unprecedented”, while stressing that “the scale is so large that it is not yet clear how it converts into actual sales.”

4. Reception and online mode: how the single-player campaign is received will shape the long tail, and when online mode arrives and how it is run will affect lifetime sales.

## Summary and a Realistic Outlook

Pulling the public analysis together:

- Conservative consensus: about 35–40 million in the first year, implying revenue in the $3 billion range.
- Mainstream range: possibly above 30 million in the first week, and 40–50 million for the first year.
- Bullish scenario: if demand keeps surging, the first year could approach or pass 50 million.

These numbers remain forecasts; the final result will hinge on launch performance, reception, marketing execution and follow-up content. Take-Two itself stays cautious, publishing no specific sales target and signalling confidence only indirectly through its full-year guidance.

Whichever range the final number lands in, GTA6 is very likely to become one of the strongest entertainment launches in gaming history. After November 19, 2026, the real sales data will emerge step by step, and only then can these forecasts be checked to see whether they were too optimistic or still underestimated the pull of this game.
