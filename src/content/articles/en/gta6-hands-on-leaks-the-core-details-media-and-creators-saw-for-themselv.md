In late August 2026, Rockstar opened up GTA6's live demo and Extended Look to press and select content creators, sparking the most detailed round of “hands-on leaks” so far. Most people did not actually play it themselves, but by watching Rob Nelson and others play on site, and through follow-up in-depth interviews, a large amount of previously unknown system design came to light for the first time. What follows is a roundup of the most reliable core leaks to date.

## Two-Protagonist Co-op: A Partner System You Can Actually Play

The most emphasized point in the demo was the real-time teamwork between Jason and Lucia. You can switch control between them at any time:

- In a vehicle, one drives and one shoots, with near-instant switching.
- When apart, you can call or text the other to come and meet up.
- In a robbery you can divide roles: one sneaks in or threatens, the other keeps watch or readies the getaway vehicle.

A relationship system exists but is optional. Shopping together, working out, pulling heists and holding hands can deepen the bond, and players can also keep it a purely criminal partnership without affecting the core flow.

## A Full Overhaul of the Robbery System

This was the gameplay change mentioned most during the demo:

- Almost every enterable store can be robbed, including convenience stores, gas stations, jewelry stores and small banks.
- Low-tier targets are not marked on the map and have to be found on your own; high-tier targets may show an estimated payout.
- Both stealth (using a Snap Gun to pick a back-door lock) and going in loud are supported.
- Safes can be blown open (fast, but may damage the contents) or cracked by finding the combination (slower, but a bigger payout).
- Police set up outside the store, making multiple exits critical.
- Loot has to go into bags, different bags hold different amounts, and it only converts to cash with the right fence.

Robbery is no longer a simple “press a button, take the money”; it is a small mission full of variables and decisions.

![A Full Overhaul of the Robbery System](/media/93821fb922f6.webp)

## Wanted Level and Criminal Profile

The demo confirmed the wanted level returns to six stars. Police record appearance, clothing and vehicle, and even whether the two are traveling together. Changing clothes and splitting up become important tactics.

The new Criminal Profile system assesses your criminal style: efficient work with few kills leans “professional,” while indiscriminate violence pushes toward more extreme ratings. It affects how the world responds, but it does not force a moral choice.

## World Size and Length

- Vice City is roughly twice the size of Los Santos in GTA5.
- The full map is about three times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2.
- A full internal playthrough at Rockstar (main story plus some optional content) takes around 80 hours.

The map uses a more realistic satellite-imagery style, and the cultural and NPC differences between regions are pronounced.

![World Size and Length](/media/7c773d6a9776.webp)

## Body and Life Simulation

Weight and muscle change with diet and exercise and affect your appearance. Working out has a minigame, and doing well earns better buffs. Sleep deprivation and long stretches on the run show on your face. You can buy pet treats in stores to feed stray animals.

## Other Confirmed Details

![Other Confirmed Details](/media/e2c89d1a850d.webp)

- Shooting: closer to a modern third-person shooter, with free aim, and some weapons can be held one-handed (the other hand carrying a loot bag). A bullet mode similar to Dead Eye exists.
- Car theft: you can use a Slim Jim to pick a lock, and newer cars may need a key cloner, so smashing the window is not always necessary.
- Interaction: you can pet, scold or study dogs, and studying animals logs them in a compendium. NPCs react realistically to eavesdropping, drawn guns and the like.
- Activities: underwater treasure hunting, jet skis, powerboats, basketball, gym workouts, go-karts/track racing, nightclubs and e-scooters have all been shown in-game.
- Money: split into bank accounts, cash and stolen goods, and dying may cost you cash.

## Overall Media Impressions

IGN, The New York Times, Famitsu and others broadly agree that:

- The density of detail is extremely high, and the world “feels more like a real place.”
- The two-protagonist design gives both crime and everyday life more layers.
- The robbery and police systems noticeably raise tension and strategy.
- The visuals and animation reach a new high for the series, with a stable 30fps lock.

At the same time, some noted that the final depth of the relationship system and the Criminal Profile still needs to be verified after launch, and that some higher-tier content may be tied to the edition.

![Overall Media Impressions](/media/a90c13a1abe6.webp)

## Summary

The GTA6 hands-on leaks collectively reveal two-protagonist co-op, a multi-layered robbery economy, upgraded wanted levels and the Criminal Profile, body simulation and an enormous world. Together they point to an open world built around decisions, consequences and immersion rather than pure chaos.

All of the information so far comes from media and creators who watched on site at Rockstar's invitation, so confidence in it is high. As the November 19 launch approaches, more in-game details may still surface. For players, this round of leaks already sketches the outline of GTA6's core gameplay: a Leonida where crime genuinely carries weight.
