GTA6 is set in the fictional state of Leonida, whose core city, Vice City, and surrounding regions closely recreate the geography and culture of real Florida. Rockstar has always used a process of compression and distillation, condensing the essence of real locations into a playable open world rather than copying them 1:1. Below are the main prototype pairings confirmed so far.

## 1. Vice City ← Miami and Miami Beach

Vice City is Leonida’s central metropolis and corresponds directly to present-day Miami.

- Ocean Beach / Vice Beach: Corresponds to the Art Deco hotel strip, white sands and nightlife of South Beach and Ocean Drive.
- Downtown Vice City: Corresponds to the high-rise financial district of Brickell and downtown.
- Little Cuba: Corresponds to the Cuban community atmosphere, bakeries and street culture of Little Havana.
- Stockyard: Corresponds to the street graffiti and warehouse nightlife of the Wynwood arts district.
- Vice City Port: Corresponds to PortMiami, serving as a cruise and cargo hub.
- Other landmarks: Sahara Arena (corresponding to Kaseya Center), the NINE1NINE nightclub (corresponding to E11EVEN) and others all have clear visual counterparts.

The official description emphasizes being “far from the 80s, yet still the sun-and-fun capital of America,” pointing clearly to modern Miami rather than the retro version of Vice City.

## 2. Leonida Keys ← the Florida Keys

Jason’s main early stomping ground, corresponding to the island chain running from Key Largo to Key West.

![2. Leonida Keys ← the Florida Keys](/media/767a79895490.webp)

- The island structure linked by long bridges, the Overseas Highway-style road and the visual inspiration of the Seven Mile Bridge are all used.
- The resort atmosphere, marinas, water activities and the description of “dangerous but beautiful waters” match the real Keys closely.
- The in-game underwater systems (oxygen, sharks, shipwrecks) further reinforce the region’s tropical character.

## 3. Grassrivers ← the Everglades

The wetland heart of Leonida, officially described as “the untameable jewel in Leonida’s crown.”

![3. Grassrivers ← the Everglades](/media/28ee8654ab73.webp)

- Mangroves, shallows, airboats, alligators and pristine swamp ecology are drawn directly from the Everglades.
- Scenes that recur in the trailers, such as alligators wandering into swimming pools and airboat chases, come from real Florida wetland experiences.

## 4. Port Gellhorn ← Gulf Coast towns (with Panama City Beach and others as reference)

Officially described as “Leonida’s forgotten coast,” corresponding to the Panhandle region of northwest Florida.

![4. Port Gellhorn ← Gulf Coast towns (with Panama City Beach and others](/media/ebd1a71cf146.webp)

- Cheap motels, abandoned attractions, an industrial port and a relatively depressed atmosphere match the character of real Gulf Coast small towns.
- Locations such as the Starlet Motel seen in the trailers reinforce that “edge of nowhere” feeling.

## 5. Ambrosia ← the central industrial and sugar region (with Clewiston and the area around Lake Okeechobee as reference)

A largely industrial and rural inland region.

![5. Ambrosia ← the central industrial and sugar region (with Clewiston ](/media/5eb0cc47b7da.webp)

- Lake Leonida corresponds to the real Lake Okeechobee.
- Sugar mills, refining facilities, country roads and motorcycle culture reflect the agricultural and industrial reality of central Florida.

## 6. Mount Kalaga National Park ← the North Florida highlands and nature reserves

A northern forest and national park region, offering a natural environment completely different from the tropical south.

![6. Mount Kalaga National Park ← the North Florida highlands and nature](/media/610bf1c6a708.webp)

- Pine forests, lakes, off-road trails and hunting/fishing elements correspond to the landscapes of North Florida and the nearby Georgia border.

## The Principle of Compression and Fiction

Rockstar has never pursued geographically accurate scale. Real Florida spans an enormous distance from north to south, and Leonida in the game is heavily compressed so that players can drive from Vice City’s neon streets to the reefs of the Keys or the marshes of Grassrivers in a reasonable amount of time. All names are fictional (Leonida, Vice-Dale County and so on), avoiding the direct use of real place names while letting players instantly recognize the prototype through visual cues.

## Conclusion

GTA6’s Leonida is a distillation of Florida as a whole: Miami’s glamour and chaos, the lazy holiday mood of the Keys, the primal danger of the Everglades, the decay of the Gulf Coast and the industrial texture of the inland, all compressed into a single freely explorable map. That balance of realism and playability is the core tradition of Rockstar’s open-world design. After launch, players will be able to experience the most complete modern Vice City for themselves in this fictional Florida, “lit by the sun yet full of shadows.”
