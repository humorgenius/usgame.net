The console version of GTA6 is confirmed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. As of now, however, Rockstar Games and Take-Two have announced nothing about a PC version, whether release timing, system requirements, a pre-order page, or storefronts. PC players will have to keep waiting.

## Official Stance: Console First

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said repeatedly in public that Rockstar always follows a console-first strategy. In a May 2026 interview he noted: “Rockstar always starts with consoles, because with a title like this, you're judged on whether you serve your core audience first.”

That statement rules out a simultaneous release. Official marketing, pre-order pages, and store listings mention only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with nothing about PC.

## Historical Precedent

The PC port timelines for Rockstar's past major releases are the most reliable reference point available:

| **Game** | **Console Release** | **PC Release** | **Gap** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GTA4 | April 2008 | December 2008 | About 7 months |
| GTA5 | September 2013 | April 2015 | About 19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | October 2018 | November 2019 | About 13 months |

Setting aside the shorter gaps of earlier years, recent titles have landed in a regular window of 12 to 19 months.

![Historical Precedent](/media/d524666f1065.webp)

## Industry and Media Estimates

Mainstream outlets and analysts broadly agree:

- Earliest possible: the second half of 2027, if it tracks close to RDR2's 13-month gap.
- Most likely window: late 2027 to mid 2028.
- Later possibility: a full repeat of GTA5's 19-month pace would push it to mid 2028.

Most observers expect Rockstar to first make sure the console version runs stably and fix major issues before starting PC port work. The PC release typically brings higher resolutions, higher frame rates, and more granular graphics options, but development resources go to consoles first.

## Why the Delay?

The main reasons include:

1. Core audience first: console players are Rockstar's traditional core audience, and the day-one experience has to be protected ahead of everything else.

2. Development resources: the scale of the open world and the complexity of its systems are enormous, and a simultaneous multiplatform launch would sharply raise the risk.

3. Commercial considerations: a console-first launch maximizes early sales and word of mouth, while a later PC release extends the product's life cycle.

4. Technical optimization: PC hardware varies widely and needs extra time for adaptation and performance testing.

Former Rockstar employees have also noted that fixed console hardware makes it easier to focus on polish, while PC requires more time to handle a wide range of configurations.

![Why the Delay?](/media/4d88819977f3.webp)

## Expected Storefronts

Once the PC version is confirmed, it is expected to launch on:

- Steam
- Rockstar Games Launcher
- Possibly Epic Games Store

System requirements are completely unknown. Given the game's scale and features such as ray tracing, high specs are expected, particularly for VRAM and CPU performance.

## What Players Can Do Now

The most practical steps for now:

![What Players Can Do Now](/media/a7cab66400a4.webp)

- Follow Rockstar's official Newswire and social channels, where any PC news will appear first.
- Watch the console version's optimization and update cadence after launch as a reference for timing the PC release.
- Hold off on high-end hardware upgrade decisions until official system requirements are published.

## Summary

There is currently no official release date for GTA6 on PC. The console launch on November 19, 2026 is locked in, while the PC version will most likely arrive between late 2027 and mid 2028. That pacing fits Rockstar's long-standing console-first strategy and reflects the practical demands of porting a major release.

For PC players the wait is long, but past experience shows the eventual PC version tends to offer better visuals, frame rates, and mod support. The real answer still has to come from an official Rockstar announcement.
