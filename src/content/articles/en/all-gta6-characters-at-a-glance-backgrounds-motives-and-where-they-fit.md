Jason Duval is one of GTA6’s two protagonists. He grew up among grifters and criminals, and to escape a turbulent adolescence he briefly served in the military. After his discharge he returned to Leonida and was soon pulled back into the local drug trade. He lives in a property owned by Brian Heder, on the condition that he helps with jobs that can’t stand the light. Rockstar describes him as wanting an easy life while life keeps getting harder. Jason knows what he wants — stability, calm, no one chasing him — yet he keeps getting dragged along by bigger plays. Meeting Lucia showed him a possible way out and made him realize the risks were multiplying. His military background makes him good in action and quick to adapt, and his smuggling experience in the Leonida Keys leaves him acutely alert to waterways and law enforcement movement. He isn’t a born schemer; he is a man pushed step by step to the sharp end by his era and his environment. Playing as him, you feel the tension of wanting to stop but being unable to.

## Lucia Caminos

![Lucia Caminos](/media/56df4a7e3a02.webp)

Lucia Caminos is the first female protagonist in the series’ mainline entries to have full voice acting and narrative weight. Her father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk, and life has been an opponent in the ring ever since. For her family’s sake she ended up in a Leonida prison, then was released by a twist of fate. Once free, she took the lesson to heart and resolved to calculate every step. Her mother had dreamed of a better life back in Liberty City, and that determination is now Lucia’s core motivation. She is done with fantasy; she intends to take her future with her own hands. The official line — “the only things that matter in this world are the people you deal with and the money in your hand” — sums up her realism. She sees life with Jason as a possible way out. After a simple robbery goes wrong, the two are pulled into a conspiracy that spans the state, and they have to lean on each other more than ever. Lucia is smart, ruthless, and ambitious, but more calculating than she used to be. She is both the emotional core and a major driver of the action.

## Cal Hampton

![Cal Hampton](/media/a7cab66400a4.webp)

Cal Hampton is Jason’s friend and also works for Brian. What makes him feel safest is staying home with a beer, eavesdropping on Coast Guard radio, and browsing the web in incognito mode. Rockstar captures his worldview with “what if everything online is real?” He is a bit paranoid and likes company, yet happily occupies the rhythm of the underworld’s lower rungs. His signature lines, “those birds fly way too neat” and “the crazies are in charge. Get used to it,” sketch his personality vividly. Cal is content where he is, while Jason is starting to want more, and that gap plants potential friction. He may serve as an intelligence source in missions, using radio monitoring to learn about law enforcement movements ahead of time. As part of the boat yard circle, he often drinks with Jason, plays mini golf, or relaxes by the pool. Cal represents the ordinary people who aren’t in a hurry to climb, yet still get pushed along by the times.

## Boobie Ike

![Boobie Ike](/media/e4965c7a8aa3.webp)

Boobie Ike is a local Vice City legend. He went from the street to a legitimate empire of real estate, strip clubs, and a recording studio, one of the few who genuinely locked in his position. Rockstar describes him as always smiling, except when he is talking business. Boobie seems to look out only for himself, yet what he values most is his work with young artist Dre’Quan at Only Raw Records. All they need now is the one track that makes their name. Club money funds the studio and drug money funds everything, a logic that lets him move easily between legal and illegal. Boobie is a key figure linking the street to the entertainment business, someone who can supply the protagonists with resources and could also become the focus of a larger conflict of interest. His presence makes Vice City nightlife and the music industry feel real and complicated.

## Dre’Quan Priest

![Dre’Quan Priest](/media/790e992bff42.webp)

Dre’Quan Priest cares more about making money than about running with a gang properly. He gets by selling drugs, but breaking into the music industry is the real goal. He has now signed Real Dimez and runs Only Raw Records with Boobie. Rockstar writes that his days booking shows at Boobie’s strip club may be numbered, because his eyes are on Vice City’s stages. Dre’Quan is an ambitious producer who needs a genuine hit to prove himself. He understands the street hustle and how to turn an audience into a business. For the protagonists he may be a key contact on music-related missions; for Real Dimez he is the one pushing them onto bigger stages. Dre’Quan represents the strivers who want to jump from the gray zone into the mainstream.

## Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy)

![Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy)](/media/f728f690b437.webp)

Bae-Luxe and Roxy have been friends since high school and perform together as Real Dimez. With their smarts, they turned their time shaking down local dealers into cold, hard cash, powered by spicy rap tracks and a relentless social media presence. An early hit single with local rapper DWNPLY shot them to wider attention. Five years later, after plenty of trouble, they have signed to Only Raw Records, hoping their luck strikes twice. Rockstar describes them with “viral videos. Hit tracks.” and “one hit away from fame.” The duo blends street experience, rap talent, and online reach into a vivid symbol of modern Leonida entertainment culture. Players will likely interact with them through social media feeds, club performances, or studio scenes.

## Raul Bautista

![Raul Bautista](/media/4f29ef8ceb69.webp)

Raul Bautista is confident, charming, and sharp, an experienced bank robber. He is always looking for people willing to bet everything on a big payoff. Rockstar stresses that “experience is capital” and notes that his recklessness sends the risk of every job straight up. The crew will eventually have to choose: double down or walk away. Raul is not a street-level thug but a genuine professional, skilled at building a crew and drawing up a plan. In the Extended Look he fled in the same car as Jason and Lucia, hinting that he could be a key figure in a major heist. He draws people in with charm and experience and tests them with escalating risk, the archetypal symbol of high risk and high reward.

## Brian Heder

![Brian Heder](/media/99f8a229a356.webp)

Rockstar describes Brian Heder as “a classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys.” These days he runs the boat yard with his third wife, Lori, still moving product. He lets Jason live rent-free at one of his properties, so long as he helps with jobs that can’t stand the light. Brian looks like a beach bum but moves like a great white shark. He is an important figure in Jason’s early life, providing housing and work while tying him into the gray chain. The boat yard is both a legitimate business and a node in the smuggling network. Brian represents the old guard who survived from the golden age to now and still won’t truly go straight.

Together these characters form GTA6’s rich web of relationships. From the protagonists’ personal struggles to the music industry’s fight for attention, to the high-risk work of smuggling and robbery, each of them survives in their own way in the sun and shadow of Leonida.
