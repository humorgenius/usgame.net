As Rockstar Games' long-awaited blockbuster, Grand Theft Auto VI has kept players worldwide fixated on one question: when does it arrive? After several adjustments, the official date is finally locked in. Here is the most accurate, most complete roundup of the release timing available right now.

## Official Release Date Confirmed

GTA 6 launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026.

Launch platforms:

- PlayStation 5 (with PS5 Pro enhancements)
- Xbox Series X|S

No PC release date has been announced yet.

Rockstar Games confirmed the date in November 2025, and Take-Two Interactive executives have reaffirmed it several times since. The company says the date is locked and the development team is working toward it as it finishes the final polish.

## How the Release Date Changed

The GTA 6 release date has been adjusted publicly twice:

## 1. Original plan: Fall 2025 (the window announced with Trailer 1)

## 2. First delay: moved to May 26, 2026

## 3. Second delay: finally set for November 19, 2026

Announcing the final delay, Rockstar said: “We know you've been waiting a long time, and we're sorry for the delay. The extra months let us polish the game further and reach the level of quality you've been waiting for and deserve.”

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has also said publicly, more than once, that the decision was the right one to make sure the game meets expectations, calling November 19 “a great release window” that still falls within the same fiscal year.

## Unlock Times Around the World

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time rather than at a single global time. That means players in different regions get their hands on it hours apart:

- New Zealand: first to unlock (midnight local time on November 19, which is still daytime on November 18 in some regions)
- Australia and other territories follow
- Europe, Asia and elsewhere open up as local midnight arrives
- The US, Canada and other North American regions come later
- Mexico and parts of Central America may be among the last to unlock

Based on public information, the gap between the earliest and latest regions can exceed 15–19 hours. Expect plenty of gameplay footage and discussion to hit global social platforms early on launch day.

## Preloads and Physical Copies

- Digital preload: expected to begin November 12, 2026 (one week before launch)
- Physical edition: the box contains a download code rather than a disc, and preloading works the same way
- Preorders opened on June 25, 2026, with preorder bonuses (including a Vice City nostalgia content pack)

With the game expected to be huge (the industry widely predicts close to or above 150–200GB), preloading early matters a lot for console players.

## When Is the PC Version Coming?

There is still no official PC release date. Based on the pace of Rockstar's past ports:

- GTA 5 came to PC about 19 months after the console version
- Red Dead Redemption 2 came to PC more than a year after the console version

Going by that pattern, most analysts expect the GTA 6 PC version between late 2027 and 2028. Rockstar has always stressed that console players are its core audience, so prioritizing the console version is standard practice.

## Why November 19?

November is the traditional holiday shopping season and a golden window for big AAA releases. The extra development time also gave the team room to polish:

- The dual-protagonist system and relationship interactions
- Leonida's sprawling map and dynamic world
- Underlying performance such as visuals, physics and AI
- Overall completeness and stability

Rockstar and Take-Two have repeatedly said they would rather take more time than ship something short of expectations. Judging by reactions to the Netflix gameplay demo, most players understand and support the call.

## What Else to Expect Before Launch

Less than two months out from launch (as of now), there may still be:

- More gameplay demos or deep dives into the mechanics
- Reviews (if Rockstar lifts the embargo early)
- Final preorder campaigns and bonus confirmations
- Console optimization details (especially for PS5 Pro)

## Summary

GTA 6 launches on consoles on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

There is no firm PC date yet, and the wait will likely run about another year.

This is the real “final confirmation.” After multiple delays, the date is locked and development is in its final stretch. For players who have waited years, the countdown has genuinely begun. Whether it's returning to modern-day Vice City or taking on Jason and Lucia's run from the law, November 19, 2026 will be a landmark date in gaming history.

All that's left is to clear space on your console, get ready for the preload, and wait patiently for the day to arrive.
