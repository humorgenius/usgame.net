GTA6 brings the natural interaction systems of Red Dead Redemption 2 into a modern urban crime world, with fishing and hunting becoming important leisure and income paths in the open world. Rockstar has confirmed that both exist, and their roles are sharply different: fishing leans toward relaxation and collection, while hunting feeds directly into the economy. Mount Kalaga National Park is the main venue, and the swamps and other wilderness areas also offer related experiences.

## Fishing: Pure Catch and Release

Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North, said plainly that fishing in GTA6 uses a catch-and-release model, and that players cannot keep, cook, or sell their catch. The reason is simple: the modern Leonida economy runs on fast food, convenience stores, and restaurants, so survival-style subsistence fishing does not fit the setting.

The core value of fishing lies in:

- Relaxation and pacing: A break from high-intensity crime and chases, with quiet time by the water.
- Challenge and collection: Satisfaction from completing specific fish challenges and filling out a compendium or achievements.
- Two-player experience: You can head out fishing with your partner, deepening the relationship.

The controls build on the feel of Red Dead Redemption 2, including casting, reeling in, and the pull feedback of different fish. Freshwater spots (the rivers of Mount Kalaga) and saltwater areas (the Keys and elsewhere) may offer different species, adding incentive to explore.

![Fishing: Pure Catch and Release](/media/91689001c37a.webp)

## Hunting: A Money-Making Part of the Economy

Unlike fishing, hunting is explicitly designed as a way to make money. Nelson noted that hunting “feeds directly into the economy” and is one of the main ways players earn income. It unlocks through one of Jason's contacts, making sure it ties naturally into the world rather than feeling like a bolted-on minigame.

Hunting features include:

- Tracking and aiming: Players can track deer, cougars, and other animals through the forests of Mount Kalaga, using rifles and other weapons.
- Study system: Animals can be studied, similar to the compendium collection in Red Dead Redemption 2, enriching your understanding of the world.
- Turning a profit: Kills can be sold for money, and rare animals may bring higher returns.
- Regional differences: The northern forests favor deer and big cats, while the swamps (Grassrivers) may involve dangerous quarry such as alligators, where risk and reward go together.

Hunting is not only about shooting; it emphasizes observation, tracking, and use of the environment, giving wilderness exploration more purpose.

![Hunting: A Money-Making Part of the Economy](/media/da5c335d2600.webp)

## Key Location: Mount Kalaga National Park

The official description states clearly that Mount Kalaga offers “premium hunting, fishing, and off-road trails.” It is:

- The main stage for fishing and hunting
- An extension area for kayaking and dirt bike riding
- A dense wildlife zone (deer herds, cougars, foxes, and more)
- A narrative space shared with “remote residents who live far from the government's gaze”

Other regions such as the swamps also support these activities, forming a complete chain of activity from the city to the wilderness.

## Design Intent and Comparison with Previous Games

Red Dead Redemption 2 made fishing and hunting a survival core; GTA6 adjusts them for a modern setting:

- Fishing sheds the utilitarian angle and returns to pure fun.
- Hunting keeps its economic value, serving as a legal (or gray) income source.
- Together they round out non-criminal play, making the world more three-dimensional.

This design avoids the filler feeling of features that exist only to exist, letting natural activities serve pacing and a sense of character life instead. Players can cast a line by the river after robbing a bank, or head into the forest to hunt when they need cash, forming a natural cycle of tension and release.

## Summary

Fishing in GTA6 is a catch-and-release leisure mode focused on challenge and relaxation, while hunting is a money-making economic activity unlocked through a contact and woven into the world. Mount Kalaga National Park is the core stage for both, and alongside the animal study system it makes wilderness exploration purposeful and detailed.

After launch, players will experience it for real: the ease of fishing with Lucia by a quiet river, or the tension and payoff of tracking prey through the forest and turning a kill into cash. Together these activities form the “life beyond crime” in GTA6's open world. On November 19, 2026, the mountains and waters of Leonida open to players, inviting you to put down the gun, pick up a rod or a rifle, and feel a different rhythm.
