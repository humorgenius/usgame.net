Real Dimez is a rap duo in GTA6 made up of Bae-Luxe and Roxy. Friends since high school, they used their wits to turn shaking down local drug dealers into sharp-tongued tracks, then turned those stories into real money through a powerful social media presence. This GTA6 character breakdown introduces the sisters who turned a street hustle into a music career.


High School Friends, Street Roots

Bae-Luxe and Roxy's friendship goes back to high school. They are not idols who started in a recording booth but realists who came up through the street hustle. Early on they built experience and cash by shaking down local dealers, then wrote those real experiences into lyrics, shot them as viral videos, and kept feeding social media. Rockstar sums up their style as “Viral videos. Hit hooks.” Their line “the whole spot's my crew. That's my sister, bow down.” captures their confidence, swagger, and loyalty.

Fame is one song away. The duo knows it, and they have been chasing that song ever since.

An Early Hit and Five Tough Years

An early single with local rapper DWNPLY briefly put Real Dimez on top. The collab let them taste fame, but it also brought trouble in its wake. Five years and plenty of hard knocks later, the two have finally signed with Only Raw Records, hoping luck turns their way again.

Only Raw Records is run by Dre'Quan Priest with backing from Boobie Ike. For Real Dimez the signing is both an opening and a pressure test: they need another track that genuinely breaks through to go from “they used to be hot” to a real mainstream act.

![GTA6's Real Dimez Explained: The Duo Who Went from Street Shakedowns t](/media/f728f690b437.webp)

What They Bring to the Game's Culture and Story

Real Dimez represents GTA6's sharp satire of contemporary music and social media culture. They fuse street experience, rap talent, and online reach, making them one of the liveliest symbols in Leonida's entertainment scene. Players will likely run into them through social media feeds, club performances, studio scenes, or mission lines tied to Dre'Quan and Boobie.

Their presence also completes the Only Raw Records storyline: Dre'Quan needs them to deliver a hit to prove himself, Boobie needs them to succeed to shore up his music investment, and Real Dimez themselves need to seize this life-changing shot while keeping their street cred intact.

![GTA6's Real Dimez Explained: The Duo Who Went from Street Shakedowns t](/media/bbc49873ba32.webp)

## One Song Away

Bae-Luxe and Roxy prove in practice that in Vice City, talent, hustle, and reach convert into one another. They came up from the days of shaking down dealers, turned those real experiences into lyrics, and turned the lyrics into a single that could change their fate. Standing at the door of Only Raw Records, they may genuinely be one song away from real fame.

Whether they blow up again or keep drifting through trouble, Real Dimez will inject the most vivid contemporary color into GTA6's music and nightlife scene. As the game's release nears, the sisters who have fought side by side since high school look set to become one of the entertainment figures players remember most from Leonida.
