Leonida is ringed by water: the Vice City coastline, the islands of the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers swamps, plus inland lakes and rivers. That makes watercraft far more important in GTA6 than in previous entries. Based on official trailers, promotional art and edition bonus information, here’s the watercraft lineup confirmed so far.

## 1. Airboat (Fanboat): The Swamp Specialist

The airboat is GTA6’s most iconic new watercraft. It shows up repeatedly in Grassrivers material, and Trailer 2 even includes a scene of an airboat being chased by a helicopter.

- Design: a flat-bottomed hull plus a large rear-mounted fan, built for shallow water, mud and vegetation-choked waterways.
- Where it fits: the Grassrivers wetlands (modeled on the Everglades), areas ordinary boats cannot cross.
- Confirmed model: the Airgator Airboat 09 and others appear in official screenshots.

The airboat turns the swamp from an obstacle into a play space you can cross at speed, and it’s the key tool for exploring the region.

## 2. Jet Ski (Seashark): The Fast Coastal Option

The series’ classic jet ski returns and appears multiple times in the trailers.

- Confirmed appearances: shown in both Trailer 1 and Trailer 2, with some being transported on trailers.
- Uses: fast coastal travel, chases, casual cruising.
- Brands: traditional series models such as the Speedophile Sea Shark are expected to return with upgrades.

Best suited to short high-speed runs around the Vice City beaches and the Keys.

![2. Jet Ski (Seashark): The Fast Coastal Option](/media/83b5038afd1e.webp)

## 3. Kayak: A Series First

The Crest Kayak is the first kayak properly added to the series, appearing in official promotional art and the Ultimate Edition bonuses.

- Role: a slower-paced exploration tool, suited to shallow water, rivers and lakes.
- Acquisition: Ultimate Edition players can get one at Jason’s safehouse.
- Locations: shown around Mount Kalaga National Park and in the Keys’ waters.

The kayak emphasizes quiet, immersive exploration, a sharp contrast with the high-speed jet ski.

## 4. Speedboats and Standard Motorboats

Several speedboats and motorboats appear in trailers and screenshots:

- High-speed boats (similar to the Toro and Speeder from earlier games) for coastal chases.
- Mid-size motorboats suited to everyday travel on the water.
- Some models are tied to fishing activities, such as the Ultimate Edition’s Shitzu Squalo, described officially as “perfect for casting a line in Gambit Bay.”

These boats cover needs ranging from leisure to escaping a crime scene.

## 5. Yachts and Luxury Vessels

As the capital of sun and pleasure, Vice City has yachts everywhere. Trailers show plenty of luxury yachts docked and underway, continuing the series’ tradition of showing off wealth while likely serving as social or mission platforms.

![5. Yachts and Luxury Vessels](/media/e4d0a7bd9ca1.webp)

## 6. Other Related Systems

- Towing: Rockstar has confirmed vehicles can tow boats, the first time the series has clearly supported it.
- Water physics upgrade: wakes affect surrounding objects, and the water surface behaves more realistically.
- Diving integration: boats can serve as dive start points, linking scuba gear and underwater exploration into a complete chain.

## Why the Design Matters

Florida-style geography makes watercraft a necessity for exploration and escape rather than an optional toy. Airboats solve the problem of crossing the swamps, kayaks provide slow-paced immersion, and jet skis and speedboats cover the need for speed. The towing system and upgraded water physics add further realism.

![Why the Design Matters](/media/8dee4bd8f8a2.webp)

## Summary

GTA6’s watercraft lineup centers on airboats, jet skis, kayaks, speedboats and yachts, covering every setting from shallow swamp to open sea. The series’ first kayak and its first proper towing support mark a new high point in how much emphasis Rockstar places on water gameplay.

After launch, players will get to feel it for themselves: in Leonida, getting around by water matters as much as driving on land. Whether it’s an airboat cutting across gator-infested wetlands or a kayak gliding quietly over the Keys’ shallows, watercraft will be a key way to explore this sunshine state.
