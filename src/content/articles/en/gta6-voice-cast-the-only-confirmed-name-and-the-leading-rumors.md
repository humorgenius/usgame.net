The GTA series has always guarded its voice cast closely, with the full list usually becoming public only once the game ships and the credits roll. GTA6 is no exception. As of September 2026, the studio has not announced the voice actors behind protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, and just one supporting role has been confirmed, by the actor himself.

## The Only Official Confirmation: Stephen Root as Brian Heder

![The Only Official Confirmation: Stephen Root as Brian Heder](/media/8acd89c60d59.webp)

On September 14, 2026, veteran actor Stephen Root confirmed in an interview with the Associated Press that he is in GTA6. “Yeah, you'll see me in it,” he said. “My character was in it from the first trailer, about a year ago. Too many people know my voice from King of the Hill, so the secret was out a while back.”

Root plays Brian Heder, Jason's early boss and landlord in the Leonida Keys. He delivers the trailer's signature line, “Just go get me my checks.” The Extended Look also shows a rooftop gathering with Brian, Jason, and Lucia.

Root is known for a highly recognizable voice. His credits include:

- Bill Dauterive and Buck Strickland in King of the Hill
- Milton Waddams in Office Space
- The recent Emmy-nominated series Widow’s Bay

He is the only GTA6 cast member publicly confirmed by the actor himself so far.

## Lead Voice Roles: Still Unannounced

![Lead Voice Roles: Still Unannounced](/media/27dbe330602c.webp)

The actors behind Jason and Lucia remain officially unconfirmed. The most widely circulated community guesses are:

- Lucia Caminos: The name mentioned most often is actress Manni L. Perez, cited for her physical resemblance, a similar vocal timbre, and her past background voice work in the GTA Online Diamond Casino Heist update. Perez has neither confirmed nor denied it.
- Jason Duval: Guesses include Dylan Rourke and others, but with far less credibility, and several well-known voice actors, such as Troy Baker and Roger Craig Smith, have explicitly denied playing the role.

It should be stressed that these are fan and press inferences drawn from appearance and voice comparisons, not confirmations from Rockstar or the actors themselves. Credits on user-edited platforms such as IMDb also lack authoritative sourcing and should not be treated as fact.

## Rockstar's Tradition of Secrecy

Rockstar has always kept cast details tightly under wraps. In previous games:

- The voice actors for GTA5's three protagonists were not widely confirmed until around launch.
- Roger Clark, the voice of Arthur Morgan in Red Dead Redemption 2, was only officially revealed after the game went live.

Recent reports say Rockstar has reminded the actors working on GTA6 of their nondisclosure agreements, asking them not to reveal their involvement before the November 19 release. Stephen Root's early slip is the exception, and it prompted the studio to remind other actors again.

## Other Actors Who May Appear

Beyond the leads and Brian, some supporting actors have mentioned GTA6 on their personal résumés, though most list only “background role” or give no specifics about their part, and none have been officially confirmed. The full cast is expected to be revealed all at once in the credits when the game launches.

![Other Actors Who May Appear](/media/a7cab66400a4.webp)

## Summary

As it stands, the only member of the GTA6 voice cast confirmed by the actor himself is Stephen Root as Brian Heder. The performers behind Jason and Lucia remain the biggest question mark, with Manni L. Perez (Lucia) the most popular community guess, though none of it carries official backing.

Rockstar is sticking to its usual low-key playbook, saving the casting surprises for last. With the November 19 release approaching, the full voice cast will eventually come out. Until then, the one thing players can be certain of is that the voice of Brian Heder does belong to that familiar veteran from King of the Hill.
