GTA6 has released three major official videos to date: Trailer 1 in December 2023, Trailer 2 in May 2025, and the 26-minute An Extended Look released on August 27, 2026. Each builds on the last, from atmosphere to character work to genuine gameplay, sketching the shape of the game in full. Here is a chronological breakdown.

## Trailer 1 (December 2023): First Look at the World

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/QdBZY2fkU-0?si=FGn_GpLit2WjCfdJ&amp;enablejsapi=1" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

The first trailer opens on Tom Petty's “Love Is a Long Road” and formally confirms the modern-day Vice City and Leonida setting. The focus isn't story but the world itself, with neon, beaches, swamps, crowds, wildlife and street detail presented one after another.

Key takeaways:

- Dual protagonists Jason and Lucia make their debut
- The map covers varied environments including city, keys and wetlands
- Visuals and physics are already far beyond the previous game
- The overall tone balances satire and realism

Trailer 1 is more of a declaration about the world, telling players this is a denser, livelier, more realistic open world. It leaves plenty unresolved but successfully ignited global anticipation.

## Trailer 2 (May 2025): Characters and Relationships Emerge

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/VQRLujxTm3c?si=BV4Rlx2bLlOjeuQU&amp;start=3&amp;enablejsapi=1" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

The second trailer turns its attention to people. Jason and Lucia's partnership moves to the center, and the official description makes clear that “a simple robbery goes wrong and the two are pulled into a conspiracy spanning the whole state.”

Main highlights:

- Clearer two-protagonist interaction and emotional tension
- The supporting cast appears as a group for the first time (Brian Heder, Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan, Real Dimez, Raul and others)
- Map regions open up further (the Keys, the Grassrivers, the port and more)
- Lighting, reflections, hair and cloth physics improve markedly
- Nightlife, street culture and musical elements stand out more

Trailer 2 gives players their first real sense of the story's weight rather than just a world showcase. It confirms the game will drive its main line through character relationships, not simply an escalation of crime.

## An Extended Look (August 27, 2026): Gameplay Lands

![An Extended Look (August 27, 2026): Gameplay Lands](/media/e48542789614.webp)

This is the most information-dense video so far. The 26-minute gameplay demo (captured on PS5) gives players their first look at actual play rather than a cut atmosphere piece.

Key reveals include:

## Dual Protagonist Switching

Players can switch between Jason and Lucia at any time during missions. One drives while the other shoots, and the character you switch away from keeps acting. The relationship system is confirmed to run shallow or deep, letting players pursue a romance or keep things strictly partners.

## Life and Appearance Systems

Diet, exercise and sleep genuinely change a character's build and appearance. The demo already shows visible sweat, hair physics and clothing interaction.

## Mission Structure

Several mission types are shown: a pickup for Boobie that runs into a police raid, escorting Raul out of a pursuit, bodyguard work and more. Transitions between cutscene and gameplay are nearly seamless, with far fewer black-screen fades.

## Open World Activities

A large number of side activities flash past, including fishing, diving, basketball, go-karts, nightclubs, street interactions and wildlife watching, hinting at an extremely dense world.

## Technical Performance

Digital Foundry and other technical outlets gave it very high marks, calling the character rendering, hair, lighting and environmental detail the best in real-time rendering this generation. The demo runs at 30 frames per second, in line with the console target frame rate.

## The Evolution Logic of the Three Trailers

| **Trailer** | **Core Focus** | **Main Contribution** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trailer 1 | World and atmosphere | Builds anticipation and sets the visual standard |
| Trailer 2 | Characters and story | Establishes the two-lead relationship and the supporting cast |
| Extended Look | Gameplay and systems | Proves playability and immersion |

From “look at the world” to “look at the people” to “look at how you play,” Rockstar completed the journey from concept to finished gameplay in three steps.

## Summary

Together the three trailers form the clearest picture of GTA6 so far: a modern open-world crime story set in Leonida, centered on Jason and Lucia's relationship, with an emphasis on life systems and two-person co-op. The visuals and physics have reached a new high, and the gameplay details have finally landed.
