GTA6's core experience still revolves around crime. Compared with previous entries, this game's criminal missions put far more emphasis on realism, strategic choices, and cooperation between its two protagonists. Based on official trailers, the Extended Look gameplay footage, and developer interviews, here is a rundown of the crime mission systems confirmed so far.

## The Main Crime Narrative Framework

The story centers on Jason and Lucia, a criminal couple. The official description reads: “A simple score goes wrong, and they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest city in America, caught up in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the state of Leonida.”

Main missions escalate from small robberies to large-scale operations. The Extended Look showed several key beats:

- A drug deal gone wrong: a pickup job for Boobie Ike runs into a police raid, sparking a fierce firefight and an escape.
- Restaurant and convenience store robberies: early co-op missions that showcase hostage control, witness management, and police response.
- Driving for Raul Bautista: a high-stakes getaway after a bank robbery, switching between the two characters to drive and shoot.
- Bodyguard jobs: an armed attack while protecting a wealthy businessman, with the two fighting on separate floors.

These missions emphasize a full “plan, execute, escape” chain rather than a purely linear shooting gallery.

![The Main Crime Narrative Framework](/media/e17156ee5726.webp)

## Freestyle Crime and Open-World Robberies

One of the biggest changes is that you can attempt to rob almost any store. Convenience stores, gas stations, jewelry stores, payday loan outlets, and even gang hideouts can all become targets.

Key mechanics include:

- Time pressure: the longer you stay, the higher the chance police turn up, and they may even gather outside and wait for you.
- Multiple approaches: you can go fast and violent (blow the safe open, lower payout) or hunt for the code carefully (higher payout, higher risk).
- Witnesses and surveillance: NPCs will film you on their phones and call it in, and security cameras record your appearance and vehicle details.
- Two-person co-op: you can go it alone or bring your partner along, and pulling jobs together helps build the relationship between them.

Loot has to be turned into cash through fences, and different fences specialize in different goods (vehicles, jewelry, drugs, and so on), a design borrowed from Red Dead Redemption 2.

![Freestyle Crime and Open-World Robberies](/media/61418d800607.webp)

## Big Heists and the Preparation System

Large bank heists return and have been upgraded. The trailer already showed the Sinfrontera National Bank robbery with skeleton masks, with hostage control and team coordination clearly on display. Several large operations requiring a preparation phase are expected, involving scouting, acquiring tools, and choosing routes.

Money plays a bigger role in the main story. Some story beats require players to raise enough funds to proceed, encouraging them to build resources through freestyle crime or side activities.

![Big Heists and the Preparation System](/media/38108256f5f3.webp)

## Criminal Profile Tied to the Wanted System

The new Criminal Profile system tracks how players commit crimes: efficient, clean robberies push you toward “Professional,” while needless violence and indiscriminate killing push you toward “Psycho.” The profile affects how the world reacts and some narrative paths, but violence the game forces on you during missions does not count against your record.

The wanted system has been strengthened in step: the six-star ceiling is back, and police track you using appearance, clothing, vehicle, and surveillance information. Changing outfits, destroying cameras, and swapping vehicles become key to escaping.

## Summary

GTA6's criminal missions move from “press a button to finish” toward an experience driven by strategy and risk. Small robberies become the core of everyday income and relationship building, while big heists carry the narrative climax. Two-protagonist co-op, dynamic police responses, the fencing system, and the Criminal Profile together build a livelier criminal world where actions have consequences.

Whether storming into a convenience store on impulse or carefully planning a big bank heist, players will write Jason and Lucia's criminal story themselves amid the sunlight and shadows of Leonida. When the game launches, these systems will put players' choice between “professional” and “psycho” to the real test.
