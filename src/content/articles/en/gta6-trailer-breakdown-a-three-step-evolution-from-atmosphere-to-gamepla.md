GTA6 has released three official videos so far: Trailer 1 in December 2023, Trailer 2 in May 2025, and An Extended Look in August 2026. Each builds on the last, moving from world atmosphere to character relationships and finally to actual gameplay and mission flow. Here is a chronological rundown of the key details.

## Trailer 1 (December 2023): Establishing the World and Its Mood

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/QdBZY2fkU-0?si=FGn_GpLit2WjCfdJ&amp;enablejsapi=1" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

The first trailer opens on Tom Petty's “Love Is a Long Road” and focuses on the variety of Leonida:

- Locations: Vice City's neon night skyline, Ocean Beach, the Grassrivers swamps, the Leonida Keys, highways and the port.
- First look at the leads: Lucia in prison, Jason living in the Keys; the two share a brief moment on screen, setting the criminal couple tone.
- World detail: an alligator wandering into a convenience store, animal control handling a pool gator, street NPCs, flamingos, dolphins, sea turtles and other wildlife.
- Technical highlights: ray-traced reflections, wet roads, and dynamic clouds and water that already look fully formed.

The trailer shows almost no gameplay; its core job is to prove the visuals and lived-in feel of a next-gen Vice City.

## Trailer 2 (May 2025): Characters and Story Framework

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/VQRLujxTm3c?si=BV4Rlx2bLlOjeuQU&amp;start=3&amp;enablejsapi=1" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

The second trailer adds a lot more story and character depth:

- The official synopsis is revealed in full for the first time: “a simple job goes wrong… and the pair are pulled into a criminal conspiracy spanning Leonida.”
- Jason and Lucia's relationship: the two interacting at a safehouse, robbing together, on the run; the Bonnie-and-Clyde partnership is made explicit.
- Supporting cast introduced: Brian Heder (who lets Jason live rent-free in his property), Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Raul Bautista and others.
- Location expansion: the cheap motels of Port Gellhorn, the Ambrosia industrial district, Mount Kalaga National Park, and the confirmed return of the Vercetti Estate.
- Gameplay hints: bank robbery (skull masks), vehicle chases, gyms, nightclubs, water activities.

Trailer 2 shifts focus from what the world looks like to who lives in it and how they interact.

## An Extended Look (August 2026): Full Gameplay Showcase

This roughly 26-minute gameplay demo is the most information-dense official material so far, captured entirely on PS5:

Mission Flow

- Running a drug pickup for Boobie Ike, walking into a police raid, then fighting through a firefight and an escape.
- Extracting Raul Bautista, with the two characters switching between driving and shooting during a high-speed chase.
- Working as a bodyguard for wealthy businessman Andres De Leon, whose party comes under armed attack.

Systems Detail

- Real-time switching between Jason and Lucia.
- The Criminal Profile system appears for the first time, and a pet dog can earn positive feedback.
- A three-way money split (bank, cash, loot) and a fencing system.
- Highly interactive safehouses: eating, taking out the trash, fixing your hair.
- Six-star wanted level confirmed.
- Quick weapon switching and melee (Lucia countering with a high heel).

## Open World Activities

A large set of side activities is shown, including basketball, kayaking, diving, weightlifting, dirt bikes, jet skis, fishing, racing, nightclubs and street brawls.

## Visuals and Tech

Strand-level character rendering, cloth simulation, ray-traced reflections and global illumination, and a world density with almost no LOD pop-in were described by Digital Foundry and other outlets as a benchmark for real-time rendering.

## The Evolution Logic of the Three Videos

| **Video** | **Core Focus** | **Type of Information** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trailer 1 | World atmosphere and visuals | Locations, wildlife, tech |
| Trailer 2 | Character relationships and story framework | Characters, conspiracy, location expansion |
| Extended Look | Actual gameplay and mission flow | Systems, combat, activities |

From “this world is beautiful” to “these two have a story,” and then to “here is how you actually play,” Rockstar used three steps to build player anticipation for GTA6 in full.

## Summary

Together the three official videos paint a Leonida that is more realistic, denser and more human. Trailer 1 sets the visual tone, Trailer 2 establishes the two-person crime narrative, and the Extended Look proves the gameplay and systems are operating at a very high level. From an alligator walking into a convenience store to everyday interactions in a safehouse to dual-character shooting under a six-star wanted level, the details all point to Rockstar deepening its hybrid of life simulation and criminal adventure.
