Official pricing for GTA6 was announced in June 2026. As one of the most anticipated games in years, its price has set off plenty of discussion. Here is the most accurate pricing information and edition comparison available right now.

## Official Pricing at a Glance

![Official Pricing at a Glance](/media/067b182b3724.webp)

## United States:

- Standard Edition: $79.99
- Ultimate Edition: $99.99

## Reference prices in other major markets:

![Reference prices in other major markets:](/media/57dcd5c0c15b.webp)

![Reference prices in other major markets:](/media/6cec29ce5e34.webp)

- United Kingdom: £69.99 for the Standard Edition, £89.99 for the Ultimate Edition
- Europe: €79.99 for the Standard Edition, €99.99 for the Ultimate Edition
- Japan: around ¥9,800 for the Standard Edition, ¥12,280 for the Ultimate Edition
- Hong Kong: around HK$568 for the Standard Edition, HK$708 for the Ultimate Edition

At $79.99, the Standard Edition sits $10 above the $70 that most current-generation AAA releases charge, making it one of the few recent titles to break that ceiling. The Ultimate Edition adds another $20 and bundles in a large amount of exclusive content.

## What the Standard Edition Includes

The Standard Edition includes the complete single-player experience, plus a pre-order/early-purchase bonus:

- Vintage Vice City Pack (available with any purchase before November 20)
- ’55 Vapid Stanier sedan and an exclusive garage
- Retro clothing and hairstyles
- Tropical weapon patterns that nod to Tommy Vercetti

Digital pre-orders also grant a free month of GTA+. The base game is a single-player experience and does not include an online multiplayer mode at launch.

## Extra Content in the Ultimate Edition

On top of the Standard Edition, the Ultimate Edition adds a set of exclusive content that runs through the main story, mainly:

- Several limited vehicles (such as the ’95 Grotti Cheetah sports car and the ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy)
- Exclusive weapons and personalized weapon variants
- Additional clothing, hairstyles, and tattoos
- Several exclusive shops and custom garages (such as Rideout Customs, Sara’s Unisex Salon, and Electric Fang Tattoo)
- Extra safehouse vehicles and related facilities
- Some story-related exclusive content and activities

This content unlocks gradually as you progress through the main story rather than being handed out all at once. The core value of the Ultimate Edition is more personalization and convenience options; it is not must-have content that changes the core gameplay.

## Physical and Digital Editions Explained

GTA6 ships its physical edition as a “boxed download code”:

- The physical package contains no disc, only a download redemption code
- Both physical and digital editions can be preloaded from November 12, 2026
- The official release date is November 19, 2026

The arrangement is mainly a response to the game’s expected large file size, making it easier for players to download ahead of time.

## The Price Debate and Market Reaction

The $79.99 Standard Edition price sparked a lot of discussion once it was announced. Some players consider the increase fair for the most anticipated release of the generation; others worry it will become a new industry benchmark and push other big titles to raise prices in turn.

Pre-order data suggests the Ultimate Edition is performing strongly. Take-Two’s CEO has said that the Ultimate Edition outsold the Standard Edition in early pre-orders, reflecting a high level of acceptance of the extra content among core players.

## Buying Advice

- Just want the full main story and open world: the Standard Edition is enough, and pre-ordering still gets you the Vintage Vice City Pack.
- Want more vehicles, clothing, and exclusive shops: the Ultimate Edition’s extras are fairly substantial, and the $20 premium is decent value.
- On a tight budget: wait until after launch to see whether a sale appears, or watch for digital discounts.

No official collector’s edition or physical merchandise version has been announced yet; we will note any updates if that changes.

## The Bottom Line

GTA6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. It is a clear price increase, though it stops short of the $100 benchmark that had been rumored. For most players the Standard Edition already delivers the complete single-player experience; the Ultimate Edition suits those who want more personalization.

Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, with the official release set for November 19, 2026. Whichever edition you choose, preloading early and getting your storage ready is the most practical advice before launch.
